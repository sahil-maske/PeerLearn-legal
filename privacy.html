Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PeerLearn ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application.

Interpretation and Definitions

  • Account means a unique account created for you to access the Service.
  • Application refers to PeerLearn.
  • Company ("We", "Us", "Our") refers to PeerLearn.
  • Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Service refers to the Application.
  • Service Provider means any third-party company that processes data on our behalf (e.g. Firebase, Cloudinary).
  • User / You means the individual using the Service.

Data We Collect

Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

  • Email address
  • Name / display name
  • Profile photo
  • Skills you can teach and want to learn
  • Messages and content you send through the app (chats, posts, comments, help requests)

Photos and Media

With your permission, we collect pictures from your device's photo library or camera when you upload a profile photo or post an image. These are stored securely via our media provider.

Third-Party Services We Use

We use the following trusted third-party services to operate PeerLearn:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database (Firestore), and push notifications (Cloud Messaging). Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary — Storage and delivery of profile photos and post images. Cloudinary Privacy Policy

These providers may process your data on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy policies.

How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To connect you with other users for skill-swapping and peer learning
  • To enable chat and notifications between users
  • To display your public profile, posts, and skills to other users
  • To improve and maintain the Service

Sharing of Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. Your profile information, posts, and public content may be visible to other users of the app as part of its normal functioning (e.g. your name, photo, and skills are visible to users you connect or chat with). We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your associated data from our systems, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Edit your profile information at any time within the app
  • Delete your account and associated data via Settings → Delete Account
  • Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data

Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your data, including Firebase's built-in security infrastructure. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
